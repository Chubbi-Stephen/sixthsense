Services & Deliverables

Precision from first chord to final master.

Structured studio packages tailored for singles, EPs, live worship recordings, and full-length albums—with transparent deliverables and no surprise add-ons.

01 · Recording

Vocal & Live Tracking

Capture performances with minimal noise floor and maximum emotional presence. Always staffed with an experienced SixthSenseMusic recording engineer.

  • Dedicated studio engineer & acoustic booth setup
  • Vocal comping, tuning inspection & harmony stacking
  • Multi-track live rhythm section capture up to 32 channels
  • Raw consolidated 24-bit WAV session handover
Starting Guide ₦35,000 / hr (2 hr min)
02 · Arrangement

Full Music Production

From a voice-note demo or chord sketch to a fully arranged, radio-ready record led by OhMyJosh and our roster of session musicians.

  • Custom instrumental composition & rhythmic programming
  • Live keys, bass, guitars & percussion direction
  • Vocal arrangement, BGV harmonies & tracking hours included
  • Preliminary mix reference & full multitrack stems
Starting Guide ₦280,000 / track
03 · Mixing

Analog-Hybrid Mixing

Three-dimensional depth, vocal authority, and controlled sub weight. Mixed in-house and remotely for artists and labels worldwide.

  • Up to 3 rounds of attentive mix revisions
  • Main mix, Instrumental, TV Track & Acapella exports
  • Vocal timing & breath cleanup included
  • Typical 4–6 business day turnaround per single
Starting Guide ₦140,000 / track
04 · Mastering

Mastering & Release Prep

Final tonal balance, translation across club PA and earbuds, and strict loudness compliance for Spotify, Apple Music, Audiomack, and broadcast.

  • High-res 24-bit / 48 kHz & 16-bit / 44.1 kHz WAV masters
  • -1.0 dBTP true-peak ceiling & Apple Digital Masters spec
  • ISRC, track spacing & album sequence cohesion
  • 48-hour express turnaround available on request
Starting Guide ₦55,000 / track
