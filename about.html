Founder & Creative Director

Ataobor Joshua — OhMyJosh

Born January 9, 1990, and active at the forefront of music production since 2004, Ataobor Joshua—professionally known as OhMyJosh—is a Nigerian record producer, songwriter, vocalist, mix engineer, and founder of SixthSenseMusic.

Across two decades in Nigeria’s vibrant music landscape, OhMyJosh has earned respect both as a creative architect for acclaimed performers and as a mentor to emerging voices—uniting disciplined acoustic engineering with deep musicality.

At SixthSenseMusic, his mission remains clear: strip away creative distractions, surround artists with seasoned arrangers, and shape records that move listeners emotionally and hold their weight globally.
